How to Use GRIND
1
Log Your Workouts

Tap LOG in the bottom nav. Hit Start Session or pick a quick program. Add exercises, log each set with weight + reps, then tap Finish.

2
Watch Your Power Signature Grow

Every session updates your radar: STR, END, PWR, AGL, REC. Your shape is unique to how you train. View it in POWER.

3
Earn XP, Break PRs, Unlock Achievements

Every set earns XP. Beat your previous best weight and you get a ★ PR. Achievements unlock automatically as you hit milestones.

4
Activate Your AI Coach

Add your AI API key below to unlock the coach. It knows your full history, stats, and PRs and gives you advice based on your actual data.

Get your key at console.anthropic.com → API Keys.

5
Share Your Signature

Go to POWER and tap Share My Signature to generate a card showing your radar + stats. Post it. Let people see who you're becoming.
